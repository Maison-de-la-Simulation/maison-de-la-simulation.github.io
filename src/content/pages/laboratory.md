Maison de la Simulation brings physicists, mathematicians, computer scientists and engineers
together to advance research through computing. A joint laboratory of **CEA**, **CNRS**,
**Université Paris-Saclay** and **UVSQ**, we develop methods, software and working practices for
large scientific computing infrastructures.

<section class="editorial-section" id="sciences-calcul">

## Connecting science and computing

Our work spans numerical simulation, parallel software engineering, programming models,
visualisation, artificial intelligence and quantum computing. This combination connects physical
phenomena, numerical methods and their implementation on computing systems.

We help scientific communities make effective use of computing resources, build connections across
disciplines and turn advances in computing into scientific progress.

</section>
