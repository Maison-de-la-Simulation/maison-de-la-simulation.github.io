The **Strategy in Computing Science (SCS)** team helps develop and implement CEA’s policies in these
fields. It works with research and computing organisations in France and abroad to build
partnerships and coordinate joint projects.

<section class="editorial-section" id="data-software-ai">

## Research data, software and AI

**Contacts:** [Christophe Calvin](__BASE__member/christophe-calvin/) and
[Guigone Camus](__BASE__member/guigone-camus/).

The team contributes to [CEA’s scientific and technical data policy](https://dst-i.extra.cea.fr/)
and to its work on AI for science and engineering. Its work on scientific software includes:

- **SOTELO:** the social and technical factors that shape how scientific software is developed and
  used.
- **ARISE:** software technologies and development platforms that use AI.

</section>

<section class="editorial-section" id="exascale">

## Exascale computing

**Contacts:** [Valérie Brenner](__BASE__member/valerie-brenner/) and
[Christophe Calvin](__BASE__member/christophe-calvin/).

The team coordinates CEA’s contributions to French and European programmes on exascale computing,
high-performance data analytics (HPDA) and AI.

- **[NumPEx](https://numpex.org/):** research on methods and software for exascale computing.
- **[EUPEX](https://eupex.eu/):** a European pilot for exascale systems.
- **Exascale France:** preparations for the European exascale supercomputer Alice Recoque.
- **[AI2F](https://aifactory-france.eu/fr/):** AI Factory France.

</section>

<section class="editorial-section" id="international">

## International collaborations

**Contact:** [France Boillod-Cerneux](__BASE__member/france-boillod-cerneux/).

The team coordinates partnerships in high-performance computing, AI and quantum computing. Its
collaborations include:

- **[AIDAS](https://aidas-lab.eu/):** AI, data analytics and scalable simulations.
- **CEA–RIKEN:** collaboration with RIKEN in Japan.
- **[HANAMI](https://hanami-project.com/):** the EU–Japan alliance in high-performance computing.
- **[ADAC](https://adac.ornl.gov/):** the Accelerated Data Analytics and Computing Institute.

</section>
