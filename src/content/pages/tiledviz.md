## Exploring large sets of results together

**TiledViz & VRWall** is a web-based tiled visualisation application developed by CEA and LISN. It
enables collaborative comparison, organisation and exploration of simulation results, on site or
remotely.

Metadata associated with each result supports sorting and analysis. Support is tailored to each
scientific community, so interactions and tools match the questions being investigated.

## VRWall: an image wall in space

For presentations, demonstrations and collaborative work, **VRWall** brings the image wall concept
into mixed reality. A headset and dedicated GPUs display and manipulate large visual content without
a physical screen.
