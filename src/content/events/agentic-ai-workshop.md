<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Artificial intelligence has become a routine research tool for data analysis, modelling and
  programming assistance. Agentic AI represents a further step: systems that can not only analyse
  information but also participate in the scientific process by exploring the literature,
  formulating hypotheses, proposing experiments, interpreting results and identifying new research
  directions. In some cases, intelligent laboratories integrate agentic AI into experimental cycles
  to coordinate, accelerate and enrich scientific work through hypotheses, simulations and analyses.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Early applications suggest that discovery cycles can be accelerated substantially when AI
  contributes to several stages of scientific reasoning. This raises major questions for public
  research: is this an evolution of existing tools or a profound change in research practice? How
  can scientific quality, reproducibility and control over these systems be maintained? What role
  can CEA play in a landscape dominated by private technology companies outside Europe?
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  The workshop will review recent advances in agentic AI for science, identify the opportunities and
  limitations of these approaches, and begin a collective discussion of strategic directions that
  CEA could pursue in this emerging field.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Presentations by CEA researchers and colleagues from major international institutions in the
  United States and the United Kingdom will illustrate recent advances in agentic AI for research
  and initial work within CEA. The discussion will also examine how agentic AI may transform the
  research process and its organisation. The day will close with an exchange on practical prospects
  for deploying these technologies in support of research at CEA.
</p>
